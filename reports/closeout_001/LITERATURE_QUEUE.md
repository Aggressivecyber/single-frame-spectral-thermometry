# Verified primary sources and remaining novelty work

Read on 2026-10-14. These are methodological references, not claims that our estimator is novel or scientifically accepted.

- [SciPy LSMR documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.sparse.linalg.lsmr.html): rectangular least squares and matrix-free forward/adjoint actions; stopping codes distinguish convergence, conditioning and iteration limits. The installed library version must be recorded separately.
- [Fong and Saunders, original LSMR report](https://web.stanford.edu/group/SOL/reports/SOL-2010-2.pdf): original algorithm reference. [Author software](https://web.stanford.edu/group/SOL/software/lsmr/) remains to be opened for the reproduction notes.
- [O'Leary and Rust, Variable Projection](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~oleary/software/varpro/varpro.pdf): pre-existing separable nonlinear least-squares methodology; do not count implementing variable projection as originality.
- [Andersson et al., Lund University publication record](https://portal.research.lu.se/en/publications/multispectral-coded-light-for-high-speed-temperature-imaging-of-a/): *Multispectral coded light for high-speed temperature imaging of aluminum combustion*, Optics Express34(7),13393–13404, published2026-04-06, DOI10.1364/OE.578636. This establishes prior multispectral coded thermometry work. Specific experimental performance should only be compared after reading the paper.
